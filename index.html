What the controls do
How it works
Each pixel has a hue. It eats a neighbor whose hue sits a little ahead of its own on the color wheel, and that neighbor turns into the eater's hue. Since every hue has something ahead of it, the eating goes around in circles and forms spirals and waves. The strip under the grid shows the color wheel, and the outlined part is the bite window.
Bite window
The range of hues a pixel can eat, measured ahead of its own hue. The right handle sets how far it can reach: narrow acts like having many colors, wide like having few. The left handle makes hues closer than that safe, which stops the whole grid from syncing into one slowly cycling color.
Mutation
Random drift added to an eaten pixel's new hue. At zero things tend to freeze; more keeps them moving but noisier.
Pickiness
When several neighbors could eat a pixel, how much the one closest in hue is favored. 0 picks at random; 100 always picks the closest.
Hunger
How many eligible neighbors it takes to eat a pixel. Higher is harder to get going but gives cleaner shapes.
Neighbors
Which nearby pixels count. Diamond counts straight lines out from the pixel; Square adds the corners. The number next to it is how many neighbors each pixel has.
Radius
How far away a neighbor can be. Bigger gives larger, smoother shapes but runs slower. Hunger's maximum grows with the neighbor count.
Speed
Steps per frame. Max runs as fast as your computer allows; the readout shows real steps per second.
Reset to
The pattern Reset starts from. Some settings keep spirals going but can't start them from noise. If Reset leads to a frozen grid, try Smooth or Pinwheel, or start loose and tighten settings while it runs.
Grid size
How many pixels across. Smaller runs faster and looks chunkier. Changing it keeps the current picture, rescaled.
Palette
The colors used for the hue wheel; it changes only the look. Twilight is a soft light-to-dark loop.
Defaults
Puts every setting back to its starting value and restarts. Reset only restarts the grid and keeps your settings.
Paint
Drag on the grid to paint with the brush hue, even while it runs.
Share
The link holds your current rule, speed, start, grid and palette settings. Your last settings are also remembered in this browser.
